01
Information We Collect
A. Information You Provide

When you register, post a ride, join a ride, or contact us, you may provide:

  • Account information: email address and authentication details needed to access the Service.
  • Ride information you post: date/time, direction (e.g., to/from airport), pickup area, destination, seat availability, notes, and any other content you include in a ride post.
  • Ride participation information: information indicating that you joined a specific ride.
  • Optional contact details for coordination: phone number or other contact method only if you choose to share it.
  • Support communications: messages you send us (feedback, bug reports, questions).
B. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Service, we may collect basic log and usage information: IP address, device and browser type, pages/actions within the Service, and timestamps. We use this primarily for security, abuse prevention, and troubleshooting.

C. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect
We do not intend to collect sensitive personal information (e.g., government ID numbers, precise real-time location, or financial account details). Please do not submit sensitive information through the Service.
02
How We Use Your Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the Service (create accounts, allow users to post and join rides, and display ride availability).
  • Enable ride coordination between users in the same ride.
  • Maintain safety and integrity (prevent spam, abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access).
  • Troubleshoot and improve the Service (fix bugs, improve reliability, and maintain performance).
  • Communicate with you (respond to support requests and send essential service or security notices).
03
Ride Post Visibility and Sharing Controls

We design the Service so that information is shared in a limited way:

  • Ride posts are visible to authenticated (logged-in) users so students can find rides.
  • Participant-only information (such as joiner lists and any optional contact details used for coordination) is restricted to users who have joined that ride, where applicable.
You should avoid posting personal information in ride text fields if you do not want it visible to other logged-in users.

SMS / Text Messaging
A. Program Description

NUCarpool uses automated SMS to notify matched riders of each other's contact information so they can coordinate shared rides. SMS messages are sent only to users who have provided a phone number and opted in to receive SMS during account registration or when submitting a ride request.

B. How You Opt In

You opt in to receive SMS from NUCarpool by:

  • Checking the consent box when creating your account, which states: "I agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, and I opt in to receive SMS from NUCarpool about my ride."
  • Providing your phone number when submitting a ride request.

Your opt-in is recorded at the time of account creation. You will not receive SMS from NUCarpool if you have not completed the opt-in.

C. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You will typically receive SMS only when you are matched with another rider. You may receive one or more messages per ride request depending on match activity.

D. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP to any message from NUCarpool. After opting out, you will receive one confirmation message and no further SMS from us. To resume SMS, you must re-submit a ride request and re-consent. You may also contact us at support@nucarpool.com to request removal.

E. Help

For help, reply HELP to any message or contact us at support@nucarpool.com.

Msg & data rates may apply. NUCarpool is not responsible for any charges applied by your mobile carrier. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. NUCarpool does not share your mobile phone number with third parties for marketing purposes.
